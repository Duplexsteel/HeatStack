How it works

Simple rules you can read, check and change. No black box, no hype.

01 · PLAN

Your coins, your budget

Pick the coins you believe in and their weights (or use the default mix). Your cash is spread over the months you choose - 12 by default.

02 · BUY

Every month, weighted by heat

Each month the bot places limit orders slightly below the market. Cheap months get a bigger share, overheated coins get skipped. Deep crashes trigger an extra boost.

03 · TAKE PROFIT

Optional, step by step

Near all-time highs it can sell small slices on the way up - or switch on Buy only and it never sells. Coins on your "never sell" list are always left alone.

Built-in safety

  • Starts in dry run: shows what it would do, places nothing
  • Caps per order and per day - one bad tick can't spend your account
  • Refuses API keys that can withdraw funds
  • Never buys a coin within 10% of its all-time high

A full cockpit

Questions

Is this financial advice? Will I make money?

No and no promises. TradeBot follows rules you can read and change. Crypto can fall a lot and you can lose money. Start with dry run and only invest what you can afford to leave alone for years.

Can TradeBot take my money?

No. You create a Kraken API key that can trade but cannot withdraw - the app checks this and refuses keys that can. The key is stored on your own machine and is never sent to us.

Which exchanges are supported?

Kraken spot today. More exchanges are planned based on demand.

What does "heat" mean?

How far today's price is above or below its 200-day average. Far below = cheap = buy more. Far above = hot = buy less or skip.

Does my PC need to stay on?

For the PC option, the bot runs while the app is open. Limit orders already placed stay on Kraken even if the PC is off. For true 24/7, use a small server or Home Assistant - same membership.

Can I pick my own coins?

Yes - any coin Kraken trades against USD, with your own weights. Bitcoin always stays as the market signal.
